# Zederia UI/UX fix report

**On the Init patch, three gold waveforms suggest three active oscillators. Only OSC 1 is on.** On PLAY, sixteen bright velocity bars dominate the page while the arpeggiator is off. In the preset library, a first-time user sees a blank table with rating and tagging controls beside it. These are the strongest signs of a template-driven interface. Each component looks finished in isolation, but the screen gives the musician the wrong impression about what is sounding, what can be edited, or what to do next.

Keep Zederia’s side-by-side oscillators, the white slice through its wavetable, and the modulation dock and keyboard that stay in place across pages. They give the instrument a recognizable shape. Spend the redesign on state, control hierarchy and legibility before adding surface detail. ## First pass · Playback state and color

### 1. An off oscillator still looks like a playing oscillator

Compare OSC 1 with OSC 2 on Init. Both show the same bright stack, white slice and amber Table segment. Both filters show full teal Mix bars, although their power switches are dark. The filter response display already gives a better answer with a subdued curve and an explicit OFF caption.

Use the module’s existing power parameter to draw two distinct states. An enabled source gets a crisp foreground trace and lit power glyph. A bypassed source gets a thin graphite preview, a hollow power glyph and a readable OFF indication. Give its mode and parameter values neutral ink. Keep those controls editable for setting up the next sound. Include power state in the wavetable display’s cache invalidation so host automation changes the appearance even when the table and position stay still. **Pass** when a reviewer can identify the enabled oscillator in a grayscale Init capture, then switch OSC 2 on and see its state update in place. [Source and filter cards](../../plugin/ui/SynthPage.cpp#L110) · [Wavetable cache](../../plugin/ui/Displays.cpp#L179) · [Filter response](../../plugin/ui/Displays.cpp#L417)

### 2. Remove decorative stripes and reserve color for signal and state

The amber stroke between power and OSC 1 is a `3 × 12` logical-pixel rounded rectangle painted by `Card::draw`. It stays bright when the module is off. The same code marks Sub, Texture, Voice, Macros and other cards. A populated FX rack exposes a second implementation. `EffectStrip::draw` paints a 4-pixel stripe whose hue rotates with the effect type. Drive, Chorus and Delay acquire three different edge colors within one lane. [Supplied OSC detail · 010529](../../../../Pictures/Screenshots/Screenshot%202026-11-20%20010529.png) · [Card header](../../plugin/ui/Widgets.cpp#L1335) · [FX strip](../../plugin/ui/FxPage.cpp#L540) · [Populated rack capture](../../build/ui-ux-review/before/patch/fx_100.png)

Remove both ornamental strokes at their shared painters. Reclaim the stripe’s width and spacer. Place the module title about 5 logical pixels after the power control, or directly on the header inset for a plain title. Preserve the lower seam, collapse affordance, power hit area and right-aligned controls. An underline that identifies the selected tab and a marker that identifies an actual route still convey information.

Graphite is already the single theme. Its oscillator token is amber `#F2B85C`, filters are teal `#4FD0BE`, effects are the clay/coral `#FF8F6B`, modulators are violet `#A993FF` and Play is lime `#96D971`. A palette-file edit alone would leave hue rotation and several direct uses of those tokens in place. [Graphite tokens](../../plugin/themes/graphite.json) · [Effect hue calculation](../../plugin/ui/FxPage.cpp#L270)

| Proposed color use | Where it belongs | Corresponding cue without hue |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Graphite, steel and off-white | Module surfaces, normal dial pointers, scales, editable values and stored modes | Pointer angle, label, recessed selection or tab shape |
| Existing warm amber | Wordmark detail, enabled power lamp and the quieter wavetable layers behind the white current frame | Filled power glyph, trace weight and frame position |
| Modulation-source color | The source and destination of a route being inspected, including its depth arc | Named source and destination, arc extent and selected-row marker |
| Meter and status colors | Output level, held peak, overload and warnings | Meter length, peak tick and explicit clip or warning indication |

**Pass** when OSC, Filter and FX controls look like parts of one instrument, a selected mode stays legible in grayscale, and the musician can still trace Env 2 to Filter 1 Cutoff in the populated patch. Keyboard focus needs its own visible outline. [Source-to-route mapping](../../plugin/ui/Context.cpp#L58) · [Modulation arcs](../../plugin/ui/Widgets.cpp#L576)

### 3. Give white and black keys a mechanical downstroke

In the supplied keyboard detail, the darker white key loses the bright front lip visible on its neighbors. The black caps have a distinct nose and cast a shadow. Zederia already draws a bevel. On a held white key it changes the bottom band from 4 to 2 pixels but mixes 55% Play green into the ivory. The black key gets a dark-green body while its silhouette and shadow stay in place. Color overwhelms the small change in relief. [Supplied key detail · 010353](../../../../Pictures/Screenshots/Screenshot%202026-11-20%20010353.png) · [Zederia with C4, C♯4 and G4 held](../../build/ui-ux-review/before/held/synth_100.png) · [Key painter](../../plugin/ui/Dock.cpp#L776)

| Surface | Resting | Held target at 100% editor size |
| --- | --- | --- |
| White key | Ivory face, narrow side seam, raised front lip around 4 px | Keep the rear pivot fixed. Extend the face 2–3 px toward the player, compress the lit front lip to about 1 px, and shade the face a neutral gray. The higher neighboring key reveals a small side wall. |
| Black key | Ebony cap over a short front bevel, with a narrow forward shadow on the white keys | Lower the cap about 2 px, shorten the nose highlight and contract the cast shadow. Keep its ebony material and its position above the white-key layer. |
| Legends | Octave and mapped-letter labels printed on the key face | Move the legends with the face. The current letter ink `#8A8F97` on base ivory `#E9E6DF` gives about **2.61:1** contrast. `#515760` on that ivory gives **5.85:1**. Use ink that meets 4.5:1 on both resting and pressed gradients. |

Drive the rendering from `PianoKeys::tick` and its existing `shown_` state, which combines local holds and processor note state. Keep `keyBounds`, black-key hit priority, velocity sensing and note release behavior intact. Cover the old and new shadow extents in the repaint region. **Pass** when mouse, computer-key and MIDI input produce the same visible travel, a neighboring white key and black key can be held together, and releasing or dragging off a key restores its resting edge cleanly. Check in grayscale at 70%, 100% and 150%. [Hit testing](../../plugin/ui/Dock.cpp#L604) · [Held state and repaint bounds](../../plugin/ui/Dock.cpp#L763) · [Existing input regression tests](../../tests/plugin/test_editor.cpp#L1056)

## Verification and handoff

Reviewed on 20 November 2026 using the Windows Release editor at revision `4393f21`. The captures cover all seven user-facing pages at 100%, held notes at 70%, 100% and 150%, and SYNTH, FX, MOD and PLAY with the repository’s Test Arp Echo fixture (`arp_fx.zpreset`). All 14 offscreen captures passed the existing `--audit` checks. Five existing keyboard, accessibility and library tests also passed. The blank library message and ambiguous bypass states were still visible problems. Mouse feel, host automation, focus and screen-reader announcements are hands-on acceptance checks for the implementation.

Run from the repository root in PowerShell. Use `before` for the baseline and `after` for the candidate. Build the Release snapshot CLI and plugin tests from the code under review. Open the images at their actual sizes.

```powershell
$review = Join-Path (Get-Location).Path 'build\ui-ux-review\after'
$cli = '.\build\release\cli\zederia-cli.exe'
$common = @('--theme','Graphite','--audit','--content','content','--settings',"$review/settings.json",'--user',"$review/user")
& $cli ui-snapshot @common --page all --scale 100 --out "$review/init"
& $cli ui-snapshot @common --page synth --scale 70,100,150 --play '60,61,67' --out "$review/held"
& $cli ui-snapshot @common --page synth,fx,mod,play --scale 100 --patch 'tests/data/patches/arp_fx.zpreset' --out "$review/patch"
& '.\build\release\tests\plugin\zederia_plugin_tests.exe' '[editor]'
```
